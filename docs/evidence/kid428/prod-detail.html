Generate an invoice for a child

Audience: Staff & admin. Prerequisites: you are signed in with an admin account, and the child already has a profile.

A billing record (invoice) charges a payer for one child. Create one from Finance or from the child's profile.

  1. Open Children in the sidebar and open the child.
  2. Open the Invoices tab, then click Manage billing.
  3. Under Add a billing record, fill in:
    • Description — what you are charging for, for example "October tuition".
    • Amount (CAD) — the amount to charge.
    • Period (optional) — the billing period, for example "2026-10".
    • Due date (optional).
    • Currency — CAD or USD.
    • Status — Pending, Paid, Overdue, or Cancelled.
  4. Click Add record. Expected: the new invoice appears in this child's Invoices tab (0 invoices counter increases) and on Finance → Invoices.

Troubleshooting

  • The record is missing on Finance → Invoices: set the status filter to All statuses and click Apply.
  • The amount currency looks wrong: this form is labelled Amount (CAD) while the center totals are in AED — confirm the currency with an administrator before invoicing.
