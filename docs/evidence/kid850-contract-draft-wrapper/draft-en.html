This Data Protection Addendum (the "DPA") forms part of the Daycare Services Agreement between KiddyApp (the "Processor") and the Daycare (the "Controller"). It applies to the Processing of Personal Data in connection with the Services.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalised terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement. In addition:

  • "Controller" means the Daycare, which determines the purposes and means of Processing Child Data and Family Data.
  • "Processor" means KiddyApp, which Processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.
  • "Data Subject" means a child, parent, guardian, family member, pickup person, or staff member whose Personal Data is Processed.
  • "Personal Data Breach" means a breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data.
  • "Process" means any operation on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, use, disclosure, transfer, deletion, or destruction.
  • "Sub-processor" means a third party engaged by the Processor to Process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.
  • "Restricted Transfer" means a transfer of Personal Data outside the Arab Republic of Egypt to a country or recipient that does not provide an adequate level of protection under Applicable Law.

2. ROLES AND SCOPE

2.1 The Controller is the controller of Child Data and Family Data. The Processor is the processor of that data.

2.2 The Processor Processes Personal Data only:

(a) on the Controller's documented instructions, including the instructions in the Agreement and this DPA;

(b) as necessary to provide the Services;

(c) as required by Applicable Law; and

(d) for no other purpose.

2.3 The Processor must notify the Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Applicable Law. The Processor may suspend performance of the instruction until the Controller confirms or changes it.

3. DATA CATEGORIES AND DATA SUBJECTS

3.1 The Processor may Process the following categories of Personal Data:

(a) Child identification and enrollment data: name, date of birth, photograph, gender, nationality, enrollment status, classroom, and registration number.

(b) Child health and safety data: allergies, medical notes, medication instructions, incident records, and emergency contacts.

(c) Child development and learning data: attendance, daily reports, observations, milestones, assessment notes, and portfolio items.

(d) Family and guardian data: name, relationship, contact details, identification details where provided, pickup authorisations, and billing contact details.

(e) Staff data: name, role, contact details, classroom assignment, and login activity.

(f) Usage and security data: login records, device information, IP address, audit logs, and support correspondence.

3.2 The Processor does not Process special categories of Personal Data for its own purposes. Health data is Processed only to support the Controller's childcare and safety obligations.

3.3 The Controller must not submit data that is excessive or irrelevant to the Services.

4. PURPOSE AND LAWFUL BASIS

4.1 The Processor Processes Personal Data for the following purposes:

(a) creating and managing the Account;

(b) providing childcare management features, including attendance, daily reports, communication, billing records, and learning records;

(c) providing support, maintenance, and security;
